Shots · version 2.16.0

Shots, espresso by the gram

Shots connects your Acaia scale and the La Marzocco Linea Micra. It weighs the shot as it pours, stops the machine at your target weight, and keeps a journal of every shot and every bag of beans. This guide walks through each page.

A typical coffee

  1. Switch onTurn on the Micra and wake the scale. The app finds both by itself.
  2. Weigh the beansPut the dosing cup on the scale. The app recognises it, shows the net dose, and connects to the machine over Bluetooth ready for the shot.
  3. Put the cup onThe coffee cup is tared and, with auto-arm on, the shot is armed. The app jumps to Brew.
  4. Pull the shotStart the shot on the machine. At the target weight the app stops it.
  5. Steam milkThe machine comes back on for steam. Put the milk pitcher on and pour: the app weighs the milk and does not mistake it for another vessel.
On every page

The status bar

The strip at the top tells you, at a glance, whether everything is ready.

Scale
Green with the battery level when connected. Amber "Sleeping" when it has been idle and the app let it rest. Red "No scale" when it is off or out of range.
Micra and its switch
Turns the machine on or puts it in standby. Green when it is on, red when it is in standby, grey when the app cannot reach it.
The blue dot
Filled: connected to the machine over Bluetooth (from weighing the beans or arming the shot until a couple of minutes after the shot, or after CONNECT FOR 2 MIN). Ring: the machine is there and ready to connect for the next shot. No dot: the machine is not answering over Bluetooth. Shots still stop through the La Marzocco cloud; if you want the faster Bluetooth stop, switch the machine off and on.
?
Opens this guide at the page you are on. The tabs along its top switch between sections. Tap it again (it turns into a cross) or go back to close it.
Cog
Opens Configuration. Tap it again (it turns into a cross) to close.
Red or amber banner
Appears when the scale is disconnected or asleep. RECONNECT or WAKE brings it back.
Red machine line
When a command to the machine fails (power, brew temperature, steam), a red line under the bar says why, for ten seconds, on every page.
Tab: Scale · the page the app opens on

Scale

A plain kitchen scale with one big number. Use it to weigh your dose and your milk.

The weight
The live reading from the scale, in grams.
Net readout
When you put the dosing cup down with beans in it, a second line shows the beans alone, for example 18.2 g net · Dosing cup. It updates as you pour.
AUTO
The small switch in the status bar, shown on this page only. Off stops the app recognising vessels: nothing is tared, armed or learned when something lands on the scale, so you can weigh anything (a pitcher with milk left in it that happens to weigh as much as the coffee cup). TARE and ARM still work by hand. It is on again every time the app starts. Greyed out while auto-tare is off in Configuration.
TARE
Zeroes the scale. If the scale is asleep, TARE wakes it first. It is greyed out during a shot, because zeroing mid-shot would stop the app from knowing when to stop the machine.

Auto-tare. Put a known vessel (dosing cup, milk pitcher, coffee cup) on the scale and the app recognises it by its weight, tares it, and switches to the right page by itself. While a recognised vessel stays on the scale, anything added to it counts as its contents (milk poured into the pitcher never reads as the coffee cup), until you lift it. Pressing TARE on an empty scale is never taken for a vessel.

Tab: Brew

Brew

The page to watch while the shot pours.

Bean line
The bean you are using and its grind setting, chosen on the Beans page. Below it, the last auto-tare, for example Auto-tared: Coffee cup - armed.
TIME and WEIGHT
Shot time in seconds and the weight in the cup. The time starts when the machine's pump comes on, a few seconds before the first drops; if the machine's Bluetooth is not connected it starts at the first drops instead and reads SCALE TIME. A bar below fills towards your target.
READY
Arms the scale: the next weight increase counts as a shot. This stops a bumped scale from starting a shot by accident. With Also arm the shot automatically on (Configuration, Auto-tare), placing the coffee cup arms it for you; DISARM undoes it.
Status line
"Extracting…" while pouring, "Stop sent — dripping…" after the stop, then "SHOT SAVED" with a grind tip such as Fast (25s) - grind finer.
TARE and RESET
TARE zeroes the scale (not during a shot). RESET clears a finished shot. Pressed during a shot, RESET asks first, then stops the machine.
Target yield
How much espresso you want, for example 36.0 g. Use − and + in half-gram steps, or tap the number to type it; the lowest is 5 g. The app remembers it.
Brew by weight
When on, the app stops the machine just before the target, because some coffee still drips after the stop. "Stops the machine at ~33.5 g · learned offset 2.5 g" means it stops 2.5 g early. It adjusts that amount after every shot.

Red warning. If the stop does not reach the machine, if the machine reports it is still brewing after the stop was sent twice, or if the scale drops out during a shot, a red line says "stop the machine by hand". If the shot ends without a stop being sent (the cup was lifted early with brew by weight on), it says "Shot ended without a stop - check the machine". These also show on the lock-screen notification.

Amber line. When the La Marzocco cloud is not serving the machine, an amber line says why and since when, for example "La Marzocco cloud needs the updated policy accepted since 3 Nov - open the La Marzocco app and accept it". Shots still stop over Bluetooth meanwhile, but there is no cloud fallback. The same line shows in the notification while the app is idle. A dropped internet connection only shows after 30 minutes.

Tab: Beans

Beans

Your bean journal. The selected bean is stamped onto every shot you pull.

Add a bean
Type the name and the grind setting, then ADD BEAN. A comma or a point both work for decimals (8,5 or 8.5).
Select
Tap the round button to make a bean the current one. It then shows on the Brew page and in History.
Grind
Tap the pencil to change the grind setting you use for that bean.
Thumbs
Mark a bean as good or bad. Tap again to clear the rating.
Bin
Deletes the bean after asking. Shots keep their history.
Tab: History

History

Every saved shot, newest first.

Time and weight
The shot time ("machine" means timed from when the machine's pump came on, reported over Bluetooth or, failing that, by the cloud) and the final weight in the cup.
Details
Date and time, brew ratio, the fastest flow during the shot, and the bean and grind used.
Verdict
dialed in when the time was in your target window, otherwise grind finer or grind coarser.
More shots
The list shows 100 at a time; at the bottom, SHOW 100 MORE loads older ones.
Bin
Deletes a shot after asking. This cannot be undone.
Tab: Micra

Micra

The machine's settings, changed from your phone.

Picture
The machine beside its name: La Marzocco's photo, your own photo, or none. Choose it under Configuration, Appearance.
Connection
"CONNECTED" means the La Marzocco cloud can reach the machine. The badge says BLE while the app is connected over Bluetooth and CLOUD otherwise. When the cloud fails, the label says why: CLOUD: POLICY NOT ACCEPTED (open the La Marzocco app and accept the updated policy), CLOUD REFUSES ACCESS (open the La Marzocco app and let it connect to the machine), CLOUD SIGN-IN FAILED (check the account in Configuration) or CLOUD UNREACHABLE (no internet). A line below repeats it with the date it started, even while Bluetooth is connected.
Bluetooth line
Says whether the machine is ready for the next shot over Bluetooth ("Bluetooth idle - the machine is advertising and connects when a shot is armed"), or not answering. It also says when the Bluetooth token is rejected or the machine is in Bluetooth configuration mode; shots then go through the cloud.
CONNECT FOR 2 MIN
Connects over Bluetooth for two minutes to show the live boiler temperatures. Between shots the app otherwise stays off Bluetooth, which keeps the machine's Bluetooth healthy.
Coffee brew target
The brewing temperature, from 90 to 96 °C. Tap a number to set it.
Steam target
Level 1 to 3, lowest to highest steam pressure. The switch turns the steam boiler on or off.
REFRESH
Fetches the latest state from the cloud. It also refreshes by itself when you open the app or this page.
The cog · Configuration

Configuration

Every setting, in sections. Tap a section to open it. Nothing changes until you press SAVE; a blank or invalid field is named and blocks SAVE, and leaving with unsaved edits asks first.

La Marzocco account
The login the app uses to reach the machine through the cloud.
Machine Bluetooth
Local control on or off, and which machine to use. Off means everything goes through the cloud.
Scale
Which scale: Acaia or Bookoo, and SCAN FOR SCALE to find it and fill in its address. Also how long it may sit unused before the app lets it sleep.
Shot detection
How the app tells a shot has started and finished. The defaults are tuned; change them only with care.
Brew by weight
The learned stop offset and how fast it learns.
Grind target window
The shot time that counts as "dialed in", for example 26-30s.
Auto-tare
Turns vessel recognition on, sets whether placing the coffee cup also arms the shot, and lists your vessels and their empty weights.
Back up now · Restore data
The app copies your history to Documents/CoffeeApp by itself each time it starts and after every shot, one file per day, keeping the newest three. Restore merges a backup back in; shots already on the phone are skipped.
Diagnostics
Opens or shares the app's log, for tracking down a problem. Logs are kept for a year; older days are also in Documents/CoffeeApp.
Help
Opens this guide at Configuration.
Appearance · About
The accent colour (here "Crema"), the picture on the Micra page (La Marzocco photo, My photo from your gallery, or none), the launch screen, and the app version.
